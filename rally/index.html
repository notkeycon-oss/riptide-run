HOW TO PLAY

DRIVEW / ↑ throttle · S / ↓ brake (hold when stopped to reverse) · A D / ← → steer
HANDBRAKESPACE locks the rear wheels. Tap it to swing the car round hairpins.
GEARSAutomatic by default. With manual set in Settings, use E to shift up and Q to shift down.
CAMERA & RESETC changes camera: chase, far, cockpit (first person), bonnet, bumper and helicopter. R puts you back on the road (+5 s).
UPGRADESFinish stages to earn credits (CR). Spend them in the Garage on engine, turbo, gearbox, brakes, suspension, weight and tyres for each surface.
PACE NOTESYour co-driver calls each turn by grade: 6 is nearly flat out, 1 is the slowest, then the hairpin. "Tightens" means brake again mid-turn.
DON'T CUTA rock sits on the inside of that corner. Keep wide or you'll damage the car.
CRESTS & JUMPSLift off before big jumps and land straight. Landing sideways hurts.
SURFACESGravel slides, tarmac grips, snow and ice barely hold. Rain cuts grip a lot on tarmac.
GAMEPADRT throttle · LT brake · A handbrake · Y reset · X camera · LB / RB gears · START pause
DRIVING TIPBrake in a straight line, turn in, then feed the throttle. Weight moves with you: lift to turn in harder.
